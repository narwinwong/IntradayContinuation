## Mechanism and prior exposure

Early information may be incorporated gradually and portfolios may rebalance again near the close. That motivates a positive early signal followed by a late long position, distinct from E17's negative five-minute signal and first-hour reversal. The cited Gao et al. paper and international accepted paper include overnight movement; this extension deliberately omits that component. It may therefore fail to capture their mechanism. Published 1993-2013 findings include 2010 and are not independent confirmation. Search snippets exposed performance and regime claims; none supplies a lab outcome or selected filter. ## Fixed policy, cash and controls

At10:00, after completion of09:59 bar, compare09:59close with09:30open exactly. If strictly greater, plan one long; otherwise stay flat. Fix whole-share quantity at floor(20% of then-settled cash divided by09:59close); no future entry price informs quantity. Primary entry reference15:31minuteopen, exit15:59minuteopen, within one session. The28-minute reference interval is not the paper's final half-hour or an auction execution claim. A quantity unaffordable at the actual assumed fill plus fee is skipped without resizing; zero quantity separate. Never discard an entered losing position.

Each independent account starts with10000USD. Reuse immutable spy_opening_cash primitives: debit purchases immediately, no leverage, no use of pending receipts, sell all shares, credit net sale proceeds ten calendar days later at next planned session. The10-day rule is a conservative assumption, not verified historical clearing. Calendar-only dates throughJuly15 may resolve credits; no sealed prices accessed. Final marked equity includes pending receivables separately from settled cash; do not call pending proceeds immediately withdrawable. No interest, taxes, distributions, borrow, deposits or reinvestment of unavailable cash.

Controls, each with its own cash account and same causal quantity time/fills/settlement: (1)always_long20%, enter every eligible session; (2)always_long10%, fixed half allocation; (3)cash, zero trading. The10% account is a fixed lower-exposure comparator, not exact matched realized exposure. No full-sample participation/volatility estimate sizes a tradable control. Report actual shares/notional/holding counts descriptively. No retrospective exposure scaling or synthetic fit.

Five fixed scenarios for all policies: zero_cost uses zero explicit fees and zero adverse slippage, but cent rounding still applies to every fill; PRIMARY uses2bp adverse slippage per side and per-fill commission max(1USD,.005USD per share); fee_double doubles that commission; slip_double uses4bp per side with primary commissions; delay_stress uses15:32open entry and15:59open exit, with primary costs. Adverse purchases round up to cent, sales down, commissions up to cent. No inferred historical fee applicability or measured slippage. Cost cases cannot be swapped after outcomes. No skipped-day or policy cherry-picking.
